3. ABOUT US

PAGE TITLE:
About SST Education Trust

HEADLINE:
Building Opportunities Through Education

SST Education Trust is an education-focused initiative based in Andhra Pradesh, India, dedicated to promoting accessible education, technology awareness, practical skill development, and lifelong learning.

We believe education should help people understand the world, solve problems, develop confidence, and discover new possibilities.

Our focus is on creating meaningful learning opportunities through technology training, educational workshops, boot camps, digital literacy initiatives, learning resources, and community engagement.

We aim to support learners at different stages of their educational and professional journeys, whether they are beginning to explore technology or seeking to deepen existing skills.

WHY SST EDUCATION TRUST?
Technology is creating new opportunities across industries and communities. At the same time, many aspiring learners need better access to structured learning, practical experience, mentorship, and affordable educational resources.

SST Education Trust aims to help address these challenges by bringing learning opportunities closer to people and encouraging a culture of curiosity, collaboration, and continuous development.

Our approach emphasizes understanding concepts, practicing skills, applying knowledge, and sharing what we learn.

OUR EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

Knowledge
We value strong foundations, clear explanations, and a willingness to learn.

Practice
We encourage learners to apply their knowledge through exercises, experiments, and projects.

Opportunity
We aim to reduce barriers to education through accessible programs and, where resources permit, financial assistance.

Responsibility
We promote ethical behavior, digital responsibility, privacy awareness, and the responsible use of technology.

Community
We believe educators, learners, professionals, volunteers, and institutions can create greater opportunities when they work together.

OUR COMMITMENT
We aim to conduct educational activities with integrity, respect, accountability, and a focus on learner development. We will communicate program details, fees, eligibility, and completion requirements clearly. We will publish information about activities and progress based on verified records.

4. MISSION, VISION AND OBJECTIVES
============================================================

OUR VISION
To contribute to a society where everyone has the opportunity to access quality education, develop relevant skills, and use knowledge to improve their lives and contribute positively to the community.

OUR MISSION
To promote accessible, inclusive, and practical education through technology training, digital literacy, educational resources, workshops, mentorship, and community-based learning initiatives.

OUR OBJECTIVES

1. Promote Education
Support educational opportunities, lifelong learning, and the development of knowledge and practical abilities.

2. Advance Technology Education
Promote learning in programming, artificial intelligence, software engineering, web development, cloud computing, databases, and related disciplines.

3. Develop Practical Skills
Encourage hands-on learning, project development, problem-solving, experimentation, and technical collaboration.

4. Organize Educational Activities
Conduct workshops, seminars, boot camps, coding sessions, awareness programs, and other educational activities, subject to the Trust's governing documents and applicable requirements.

5. Expand Access to Learning
Explore free programs, affordable courses, scholarships, and need-based educational assistance, subject to available resources and eligibility.

6. Promote Digital Literacy
Help learners develop the knowledge required to use digital tools, online resources, and technology responsibly.

7. Encourage Innovation
Promote creativity, research, technical exploration, and the responsible application of emerging technologies.

8. Support Mentorship
Encourage educators, professionals, and volunteers to contribute their experience and knowledge to learner development.

9. Build Educational Partnerships
Explore lawful collaborations with educational institutions, community organizations, professionals, and other suitable partners.

10. Improve Career Readiness
Support technical communication, teamwork, portfolio development, problem-solving, and career awareness.

11. Develop Educational Resources
Create and share learning materials, tutorials, practice exercises, project guides, and other educational resources.

12. Promote Responsible Technology
Encourage awareness of cybersecurity, privacy, accessibility, ethical AI, and responsible digital citizenship.

13. Support Community Development
Use educational initiatives to contribute positively to communities, particularly where access to learning opportunities is limited.

14. Maintain Responsible Governance
Manage the Trust's resources and activities in accordance with its executed governing documents and applicable laws, applying resources toward its approved purposes.

F. LEARNER TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------

Recommended page: Courses > Technical Requirements
Publish course-specific requirements on each course page. The following are indicative
planning guidance only; verify against the actual tools and versions used.

General setup:
- A laptop or desktop is strongly recommended for programming and cloud labs.
- Indicative baseline: a reasonably modern 64-bit computer, 8 GB RAM, stable internet,
  updated browser, keyboard, and sufficient free storage. Some IDEs, containers, virtual
  machines, and local AI tools may require 16 GB RAM or more.
- Windows, macOS, or a supported Linux distribution may work, but exact support depends
  on the course tools. A phone alone may not be sufficient for full development exercises.
- Learners should have permission to install required software on their device.
- Provide a list of required software, supported versions, approximate download sizes,
  and a pre-course setup guide.
- Where a learner cannot meet the requirements, state whether a browser-based environment,
  lab computer, loan device, or alternative activity is available. Do not promise one unless
  it exists.

Course-specific notes:
- Python/Java/Go: list the supported runtime or JDK version, editor/IDE options, terminal
  requirements, and whether installation is required.
- React/Angular/full-stack: list the supported Node.js version, package manager, browser,
  code editor, and whether a local backend/database is required.
- AWS/Azure/Google Cloud: state whether learners need their own account, whether a sandbox
  is provided, and what billing safeguards apply. Never imply that cloud usage is free
  unless the current service terms confirm it.
- DevOps/CI/CD: disclose whether Docker, virtualisation, Git, a Linux shell, or a cloud
  account is required; note possible hardware and permissions limitations.
- AI/Generative AI: specify whether exercises use hosted APIs, paid services, local models,
  or browser tools; explain account, usage-limit, privacy, and possible-cost considerations.

Cloud cost and safety warning:
“Cloud platforms and AI services may charge for usage. Free tiers, credits, quotas, and
service terms can change and may require a payment method. Learners must review the
provider’s current terms, set budget alerts where available, avoid publishing credentials,
and shut down or delete billable resources after labs. SST Education Trust will clearly
state whether it provides a managed sandbox or expects learners to use their own account.”
